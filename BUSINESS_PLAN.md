# First customer plan

## What you have

A working free starter that demonstrates sales-data cleaning and reporting. It is a portfolio tool, not a finished subscription business. AI helped build it; you still need to understand its behaviour and check all customer deliverables.

## Small first offer

Offer to adapt the existing app to one client's CSV format and provide a report. A suggested pilot price is £25–£50 for a tightly scoped job; this is a pricing experiment, not evidence of market rates or an income forecast. Include one CSV format, one currency, one report and one revision. Agree the row count and deadline before accepting work. Avoid complicated refunds, tax calculations, accounting integrations or unknown data formats at first.

## Before advertising

- Run the app and reproduce the sample totals in README.md.
- Export the report and inspect it yourself.
- Take screenshots using fictional data.
- Understand how invalid rows and duplicates are treated.
- Set up a payment/service platform separately; this project has no checkout.
- Add your actual contact or service-listing URL to the repository description when ready.

## Where to look

Use the repository as evidence when applying to small spreadsheet/reporting jobs, or show the demo to a small business you already know. Ask whether they have a repeated reporting task before proposing software. Don't assume every seller wants another app.

## Order workflow

1. Ask for column names and an anonymised sample; agree scope and payment terms.
2. Confirm what revenue means and whether refunds or multiple currencies exist.
3. Make a separate working copy for the customer; keep their data out of the public repository.
4. Compare report totals against the client's existing totals and investigate differences.
5. Deliver agreed files and instructions, collect feedback and complete any agreed revision.

## Money example

Two £40 jobs = £80 gross revenue, before platform/payment fees, refunds, expenses and applicable taxes. Zero customers = £0. Track the hours each job takes so you can assess whether the price is worthwhile.

## Later improvements only after demand

Order IDs for order-level metrics; explicit refund handling; fixed-format exports for a particular seller platform; customer-specific report branding. A hosted subscription requires additional security, hosting, payment integration and maintenance and is outside this starter.
